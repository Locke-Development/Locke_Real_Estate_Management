# LOCKE REAL ESTATE MANAGEMENT
### Turn the contract into coordinated action.

**Product preview · In development · Locke Development**

A real-estate transaction moves on contract language: contingency dates, financing milestones, notices, required documents, and shifting obligations. Locke Real Estate Management is designed to give agents, brokers, and transaction coordinators a connected operating picture — grounded in the documents governing the deal.

## The difference is the handoff from analysis to execution

**01 / Understand the agreement.** Ingest purchase agreements, amendments, addenda, disclosures and notices; identify important operational terms and preserve links to their source material.

**02 / Keep the evidence attached.** Display proposed findings beside the originating clause or page, with confidence and review state rather than an unexplained AI assertion.

**03 / Put deadlines to work.** Turn approved contractual dates and obligations into accountable assignments, reminders, escalation and calendar coordination.

**04 / Make amendments operational.** Preview changed terms and how they affect previously approved deadlines, work queues, and notifications; apply consequential changes only after review.

**05 / Give brokers a command view.** Collect time-critical exceptions, missing information, and pending approvals across transactions, personnel and offices.

## Designed for real brokerage roles

| Role | Intended experience |
|---|---|
| Agent | Deal status, document review, actionable next steps, client-safe communication drafts. |
| Transaction coordinator | Document completeness, deadline ownership, closing progression and follow-ups. |
| Managing broker | Exception queues, non-standard clauses, approval decisions and office-level visibility. |
| Brokerage administrator | Team structure, access, routing, configuration and operational reporting. |

## Nationwide architecture, transparent coverage

Support for transactions in all 50 states and Washington, DC is a core architectural requirement, alongside a versioned state-form and rule coverage registry. This is not a claim that every jurisdiction's legal analysis or association form is already validated.

## Planned integration direction

Google Calendar and Microsoft Outlook; transactional email and notifications; document intake; e-sign envelope status/deep links; brokerage-system integrations via authorized APIs; and separate administrative and billing functionality. Specific integrations remain subject to product development and authorized provider access.

## Where the product fits

Locke Real Estate Management is **transaction intelligence and execution infrastructure**, designed to complement a brokerage's existing systems. Its initial direction excludes replacing CRM, lead generation, IDX listings, and native electronic-signing services.
